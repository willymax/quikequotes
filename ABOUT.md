The Problem

Home-service owners (painters, cleaners, pressure washers, landscapers, HVAC techs) and many small businesses lose jobs in the gap between "lead comes in" and "quote gets accepted," and they know it. Three specific bleeding points:

Slow quotes lose jobs. The owner is on a ladder all day, so quotes get written at 9pm or three days later. In this market, the first professional quote often wins — homeowners get 3 bids and momentum matters. Owners feel this pain acutely but their current fix is "try harder."

Nobody follows up. Most solo operators send a quote and then... nothing. No reminder, no "any questions?" text. Jobs silently go to the competitor who called back twice. Follow-up is the highest-ROI activity in their business and the one they're worst at, because it feels awkward and they're busy.

Quotes look amateur. A price texted as "itll be around 450" loses to a branded PDF with photos, line items, and a "click to accept + pay deposit" button — even at a higher price. Professionalism is a pricing lever they don't realize they have.

The existing solutions are full field-service suites — Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan — which cost $100–300+/month, take weeks to learn, and bundle scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, payroll. A huge share of the 5M+ US home-service businesses are 1–5 person operations who look at Jobber, feel overwhelmed, and go back to texting prices. That's your wedge: not a suite, just "win more of the quotes you already send."

The Solution

A mobile-first quoting + automated follow-up tool, $29–$79/month, self-serve. Core loop:

3-minute quote from the driveway. Trade-specific templates (interior paint job, standard house wash, biweekly cleaning) with pre-built line items so they're not typing on a phone keyboard. Add photos taken on-site. Crucially, support good/better/best tiered options — this is a proven mechanic that raises average job value 15–30% and it's a killer demo in a video.
The client gets a link, not an attachment. Branded web quote, photos, tiers, e-signature, "Accept" button. You see when they opened it (open tracking = "call them now, they're looking at it" notifications — owners love this).
Follow-up runs on autopilot. The moment a quote goes out, a sequence starts: Day 1 SMS "any questions?", Day 3 email, Day 7 "we're booking up for [month]" nudge. Stops automatically when they reply or accept. This single feature is the whole ROI story — it recovers jobs they were already going to lose, and one recovered $400 job pays for a year of your software.

Later modules (don't build these in v1): deposit collection on acceptance, missed-call text-back, review requests after job completion. Missed-call text-back is a great expansion because it's another "money you're already losing" story.

Positioning line: "Jobber is for running your whole business. [YourTool] is for winning the job in the first place." Start with one or two trades — painting and pressure washing are ideal because jobs are high-ticket, quotes are competitive, and both have huge YouTube ecosystems — then expand templates to other trades once the engine works.
